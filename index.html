04 — ABOUT & EXPERTISE

Where creativity meets technology.

I'm a multidisciplinary creative professional with a background in graphic design, web design, and front-end development. My journey started with visual communication and evolved into building digital experiences through design and technology. I enjoy turning ideas into purposeful visuals, engaging websites, and practical digital solutions. I value thoughtful design, clean execution, continuous learning, and meaningful collaboration.

01 / Graphic Design & Visual Communication

Creating brand visuals, marketing graphics, social media content, email campaigns, e-commerce presentations, product labels, and print-ready production artwork. Experienced in preparing technical artwork with cutlines, perforation lines, and production specifications.

02 / Web Design & WordPress Development

Transforming design concepts from AI Designs, Figma, and other design sources into functional WordPress websites. Experienced with Divi, Elementor, Beaver Builder, Avada, responsive layouts, and website customization.

03 / Front-End Development & Website Support

Working with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP to customize and maintain websites. Familiar with theme and plugin updates, website QA, SFTP/SSH, troubleshooting, performance improvements, and website security maintenance.

04 / Team Leadership & Collaboration

Experience coordinating creative and technical teams across graphic design, web development, SEO, and content production. Comfortable managing workflows, organizing deliverables, supporting teammates, and working toward project deadlines.
